BiteWindow Vector7 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Developer: 49Caliber Labs (Solano County, California)

Core Commitment: Zero Advertising, Zero User Data Monetization

BiteWindow Vector7 was engineered by 49Caliber Labs for serious anglers and mariners. We operate on a strict, transparent One-Time Lifetime Purchase ($9.99 flat) with free lifetime updates, ZERO recurring subscriptions, and ABSOLUTELY NO ADS, NO TRACKERS, AND NO THIRD-PARTY MARKETING SDKs. We never sell, rent, or trade your data to marketing firms or data brokers.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Account Sign-Ups: You do not need to register an email, password, or phone number to access all core bite window calculations.
  • No Secret GPS Tracking: Your device GPS coordinates are processed locally on your hardware to identify nearby USGS stations and solunar celestial angles. Your exact fishing spots ("Honey Holes") never leave your phone unless you explicitly trigger an off-grid LoRa broadcast.
  • No Commercial Analytics or Telemetry: We do not track what you search, how many hours you fish, or what tackle you browse.

2. Device Permissions & Why They Are Requested

  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS): Used strictly on-device to center tactical nautical cartography, sort nearest USGS/NOAA telemetry gages, and scan local Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) LoRa transceivers on Android. If denied, you can manually select any state, county, or waterbody offline.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE GATT), USB-Serial OTG & Connected-Device Foreground Service: Optional local radio bridge permissions used solely when pairing with a user-owned 915 MHz Meshtastic/MeshMule LoRa transceiver for off-grid telemetry.
  • Local Storage & IndexedDB: Saves your selected watershed, personal catch logs, waypoints, and offline forecast snapshots strictly on your device. Uninstalling removes all local data.

3. Non-Commercial Bait & Tackle Guidance

Links provided in the Bait & Tackle section to retailers (such as Bass Pro Shops, Tackle Warehouse, FishUSA, or Cabela's) are provided strictly as an informational convenience for visual confirmation and SKU lookup. BiteWindow receives no affiliate commissions from these search links and embeds zero retailer tracking pixels.

4. Data Sovereignty & Catch Log Privacy

Your personal catch logs and waypoints are stored strictly in your device's local database. You remain in sole control of all waypoints and field logs.
