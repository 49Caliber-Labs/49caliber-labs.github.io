BiteWindow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026
Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Core Commitment: Zero Advertising, Zero User Data Monetization

BiteWindow was engineered by anglers, for anglers. We operate on a strict, transparent Single One-Time Purchase model ($10–$15 flat). We charge once for the software, charge ZERO recurring subscriptions, and deploy ABSOLUTELY NO ADS, NO TRACKERS, AND NO THIRD-PARTY MARKETING SDKs. We never sell, rent, or trade your data to marketing firms or data brokers.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Account Sign-Ups: You do not need to register an email, password, or phone number to access all core bite window calculations.
  • No Secret GPS Tracking: Your device GPS coordinates are processed locally on your hardware to identify nearby USGS stations and solunar celestial angles. Your exact fishing spots ("Honey Holes") never leave your phone.
  • No Commercial Analytics or Telemetry: We do not track what you search, how many hours you fish, or what tackle you browse.

2. Device Permissions & Why They Are Requested

  • Precise Location (GPS): Used solely inside the browser/applet to center the cartography and sort nearest USGS streamflow gages. If permission is denied, BiteWindow functions identically by allowing you to manually pick any state, county, or waterbody from the public directory.
  • Local Storage & IndexedDB (Cache): Used to save your selected county, favorited spots, offline forecast snapshots, and tackle preferences directly on your physical device.

3. Non-Commercial Bait & Tackle Guidance

Links provided in the Bait & Tackle section to retailers (such as Bass Pro Shops, Tackle Warehouse, FishUSA, or Cabela's) are provided strictly as an informational convenience to our users for visual confirmation and inventory checks. BiteWindow receives no revenue, commissions, or affiliate payouts from these search links, and embeds zero retailer tracking pixels.

4. Data Sovereignty & Catch Log Privacy

Your personal catch logs are stored entirely in your device's local database. When choosing to log a catch, you have the option to keep your exact GPS coordinate private (Honey Hole Shield), masking your secret fishing spots before any community post.
