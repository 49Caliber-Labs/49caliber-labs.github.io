Privacy Policy: Branham Hills Legacy Football Pool & Open-Source Umpire Pool

Effective Date: November 17, 2026 • Applies to Android, iOS & Web/PWA Releases
Zero Ads Ever • Zero Behavioral Tracking • Entertainment Only

49Caliber Labs builds and maintains Branham Hills Legacy Football Pool (com.caliber49labs.nflpool) and the open-source Umpire Pool. This policy explains what limited data the application stores, how it is protected, and how users or League Commissioners can delete records at any time.

1. Zero-Ad & Zero-Tracking Guarantee

  • No Third-Party Advertising SDKs: Neither the Android APK/AAB, iOS release, nor Web/PWA includes ad networks, behavioral ad brokers, or cross-app advertising identifiers.
  • No Data Selling: 49Caliber Labs never sells, rents, or monetizes user pick histories, league rosters, or chat messages.

2. Strict Non-Gambling & Entertainment Policy

This software operates strictly as a recreational straight-up NFL pick’em ledger, weekly scoresheet organizer, and social bragging-rights tracker. The application processes zero real-money wagers, collects zero entry fees, holds zero escrow balances, and awards zero monetary payouts. Optional Penalty Jar tracking uses purely honorary Penalty Flags with zero cash value.

3. Information Stored Within a League Room

  • Local Device Storage: League preferences, edition configuration, theme selection, and cached schedules are stored locally on your device via browser/WebView storage.
  • League Roster & Pick Submissions: When syncing with a Commissioner-configured backend or private league room (?league=<code>), the server stores your chosen contender display name, weekly NFL straight-up team selections, Monday Night tiebreaker number, optional contact label if entered by your Commissioner, and optional Locker Room messages posted by you.
  • Transport Security: All network communication with cloud sync endpoints occurs over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Users and League Commissioners maintain direct control over league data:

  • In-App Control: Commissioners can edit or remove contenders, delete Locker Room chat messages, export JSON backups, or clear pick ledgers directly inside the Admin Hub. Local data can be cleared anytime from your device browser or app storage settings.
  • Direct Deletion Requests: To request permanent removal of any contender record, chat message, or hosted league room state stored by 49Caliber Labs, email jthreet@49caliber.com with your league code and display name. Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 days.
