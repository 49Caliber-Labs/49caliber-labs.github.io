Privacy Policy & Offline Telemetry Governance

Effective Date: November 13, 2026 • 49Caliber Labs (Solano County, California)

1. Edge-First, Zero-Tracking Architecture

49Caliber Labs builds offline-first mobile applications (including BiteWindow Vector7) and open-source sub-GHz mesh telemetry protocols (MeshMule-Core). We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal user data, location histories, or behavioral telemetry.

2. Hardware Permissions (Bluetooth LE, USB OTG, & Location)

  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE GATT) & USB-Serial OTG: Used strictly on-device to pair with local 902–928 MHz / 868 MHz LoRa transceivers and barometric sensors.
  • Geolocation & Barometric Sensors: Processed locally on the user's device to compute environmental pressure gradients (∇P), tidal harmonics, and local mesh coordinates. Coordinates are only transmitted over RF mesh when explicitly initiated by the operator.

3. Data Retention & Local Storage

Field logs, waypoints, and sensor snapshots are persisted locally on your device (`IndexedDB` / SQLite). Uninstalling the application or clearing local storage permanently removes all on-device data.
