Custom Antenna Design & Receive-Only Broadband RF Telemetry

Off-the-shelf rubber-duck whips bleed link budget before telemetry even leaves the enclosure. 49Caliber Labs designs application-matched custom radiating elements and receive-only (RX-Only) wideband RF front-ends for coastal estuaries, wildfire corridors, and sparse-cellular recreation lands.

Architecture 01 • Zero-TX Spectral Hygiene

Receive-Only Broadbanding

Federal park installations, utility rights-of-way, and shared aviation/marine corridors frequently restrict active RF emissions. Receive-only broadband telemetry decouples passive environmental sensing from transmitter licensing bottlenecks:

  • Wideband Passive Capture: Swept HF/VHF/UHF reception (10 MHz – 1.2 GHz) for NOAA weather broadcasts, AIS marine beacons, GOES DCP downlinks, and ambient RF noise-floor baselines.
  • Low-Noise Front-End Conditioning: Band-selective SAW / cavity pre-filtering paired with sub-1 dB noise-figure LNAs to pull weak beacon frames out of urban Vallejo or industrial Mare Island interference.
  • Zero-Emission Audit Profile: Passive nodes generate zero unintended out-of-band spurs, simplifying multi-agency co-location on shared fire lookouts and ridge repeaters.
Architecture 02 • Physical Match

Custom Antenna Engineering

Every MeshMule and field telemetry node is swept on a Vector Network Analyzer (50 Ω reference plane) inside its actual weatherproof radome and mounting mast configuration:

  • 902–928 MHz Sub-GHz ISM Arrays: Elevated ground-plane quarter-wave whips, stacked collinear fiberglass verticals (VSWR < 1.3:1), and directional canyon Yagi feeds.
  • Wideband Discone & Fat-Dipole RX Elements: Tapered-skirt geometries maintaining stable impedance across multi-octave receive-only sweeps without mechanical band switching.
  • Marine & Wildfire Hardening: UV-stabilized fiberglass shrouds, N-type bulkhead gaskets, and DC-grounded lightning arrestor stubs engineered with Orion General Contracting field rigs.

Open-Source Codec + Hardware Integration

All packet serialization (14-byte compact hydro/baro frames), link tomography scoring, and phase-array storm tracking live inside our MIT-licensed MeshMule-Core repository on GitHub, engineered to pair with physical tower mounts and off-grid power enclosures deployed alongside Orion General Contracting, Inc.
